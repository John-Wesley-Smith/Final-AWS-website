About the Club

The goal of this club is to help students understand AWS Cloud and its diverse use cases, including security, AI, and business analytics. It highlights the benefits of cloud computing and how it drives business growth and transformation. Through hands-on projects, students gain practical experience and build in-demand technical and business skills.

🎯

Our Purpose

The AWS Club aims to teach AWS services and tools and help students upskill for the current tech industry.

🚀

Activities

The AWS Club conducts events, workshops, and bootcamps to provide hands-on experience with AWS services, along with online sessions.
